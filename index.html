GREENHOUSE GASES

Know the Major Gases

Different greenhouse gases have different sources and environmental effects.

🟢

Carbon Dioxide

CO₂ is mainly produced by fossil fuel combustion, industry and deforestation.

🔵

Methane

CH₄ comes from agriculture, livestock, landfills and fossil fuel production.

🟣

Nitrous Oxide

N₂O is associated with agriculture, fertilizers and industrial processes.

🟠

Water Vapour

Water vapour is a natural greenhouse gas that plays an important role in Earth's climate.
