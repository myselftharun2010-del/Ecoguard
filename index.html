Greenhouse Gases

Important gases that contribute to the greenhouse effect.

💨

Carbon Dioxide

CO₂

Released mainly through fossil fuel combustion, industrial processes and land-use changes.

🐄

Methane

CH₄

Produced by agriculture, livestock, landfills and fossil fuel production.

🌾

Nitrous Oxide

N₂O

Associated with agricultural soils, fertilizers and several industrial activities.

🏭

Industrial Gases

F-Gases

Certain industrial gases can have very high greenhouse warming effects.

Prevention

Simple ways to reduce your environmental impact.

🚲

Use Public Transport

Choose public transport, cycling or walking when possible to reduce vehicle emissions.

⚡

Save Electricity

Turn off unused appliances and choose efficient electrical devices.

🌳

Protect Forests

Forests absorb carbon dioxide and support healthy ecosystems.

♻️

Reduce Waste

Reuse products, recycle materials and avoid unnecessary consumption.

🥗

Consider Food Choices

Reducing high-emission food consumption can help lower your environmental footprint.

✈️

Reduce Unnecessary Flights

Consider trains, buses or virtual meetings when practical alternatives are available.
