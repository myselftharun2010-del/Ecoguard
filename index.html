LEARN THE BASICS

Understanding Carbon

Carbon is an important chemical element found throughout nature and in many materials.

⚛️

What is Carbon?

Carbon is a naturally occurring chemical element with the symbol C. It is present in living organisms, fuels, soil and many natural and manufactured materials.

💨

Carbon Emissions

Carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases can be released through activities such as burning fossil fuels, transportation and industrial processes.

🏭

Major Sources

Transportation, electricity generation, industrial activities and land-use changes are important sources of greenhouse gas emissions.

🌡️

Environmental Impact

Increased greenhouse gas concentrations can contribute to climate change and affect ecosystems and weather patterns.

ATMOSPHERIC GASES

Important Gases

Learn about important gases and their chemical formulas.

💨

Carbon Dioxide

CO₂

Definition

Carbon dioxide is a gas containing one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms. It is produced naturally and through activities such as burning fossil fuels.

Chemical Structure

O = C = O

Prevention Methods

  • Use energy efficiently.
  • Increase renewable energy use.
  • Reduce unnecessary fuel consumption.
  • Protect and restore forests.
🔥

Methane

CH₄

Definition

Methane is a simple hydrocarbon containing one carbon atom and four hydrogen atoms. It can be released from natural and human activities.

Chemical Structure

H
│
H — C — H
│
H

Prevention Methods

  • Reduce leaks from gas systems.
  • Improve waste management.
  • Capture methane where appropriate.
  • Improve agricultural practices.
⚠️

Carbon Monoxide

CO

Definition

Carbon monoxide is a gas made of one carbon atom and one oxygen atom. It can form when carbon-containing fuels burn incompletely.

Chemical Structure

C ≡ O

Prevention Methods

  • Maintain combustion equipment properly.
  • Use good ventilation.
  • Improve fuel combustion efficiency.
  • Use appropriate emission-control systems.
❄️

Hydrofluorocarbons

HFCs

Definition

Hydrofluorocarbons are a group of synthetic gases that have been used in some refrigeration and air-conditioning applications.

Chemical Structure

CₓHᵧFᵢ

Prevention Methods

  • Prevent refrigerant leaks.
  • Maintain cooling systems properly.
  • Use lower-impact alternatives where suitable.
  • Recover and manage refrigerants responsibly.
