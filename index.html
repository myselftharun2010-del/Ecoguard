Why Carbon Awareness Matters

Understanding carbon emissions is the first step towards creating a cleaner and more sustainable future.

🌍

Climate

Excess greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere and contribute to climate change.

🏭

Industry

Energy production and industrial activity contribute significantly to global emissions.

🌱

Sustainability

Small changes in everyday behaviour can help reduce environmental impact.

🌫️ GREENHOUSE GASES

Major Greenhouse Gases

Different gases contribute to the greenhouse effect in different ways.

🌫️

Carbon Dioxide

CO₂ is released mainly through fossil fuel combustion, industry and land-use change.

🐄

Methane

CH₄ is associated with agriculture, livestock, waste and fossil fuel production.

🌾

Nitrous Oxide

N₂O is released from agricultural activities, fertilisers and several industrial processes.

☁️

Water Vapour

Water vapour is a naturally occurring greenhouse gas and plays an important role in climate feedback.
