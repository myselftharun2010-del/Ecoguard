Climate Science

Greenhouse Gases

Different gases contribute to the greenhouse effect in different ways.

CO₂

Carbon Dioxide

Released mainly from fossil fuel combustion, industrial activity and deforestation.

CH₄

Methane

Produced by agriculture, waste management, fossil fuel extraction and livestock.

N₂O

Nitrous Oxide

Associated with agricultural soils, fertilizers and industrial processes.

💨

Other Gases

Other greenhouse gases can also contribute to atmospheric warming.
